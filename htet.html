HTET Previous Year Papers – Preparation Guide

The Haryana Teacher Eligibility Test (HTET) is conducted by the Board of School Education Haryana (BSEH). Previous year question papers can help candidates practise questions, review their preparation and become familiar with the format of the examination.

HTET Levels: Understand the Difference

Level I – PRT

This level is associated with teaching Classes I to V. Candidates should check the current official eligibility rules and syllabus.

Level II – TGT

This level is associated with teaching Classes VI to VIII. Review the relevant subject requirements and official syllabus.

Level III – PGT

This level is associated with postgraduate teacher posts. Check the applicable subject and eligibility requirements in official notifications.

The descriptions above are a general guide. Always verify the latest eligibility criteria, syllabus and examination instructions published by BSEH before applying.

How to Prepare Using Previous Year Papers

  1. Read the latest official HTET syllabus for your intended level.
  2. Open a previous year paper from the relevant year and level.
  3. Attempt the questions under timed conditions.
  4. Review your answers and note the topics that need further revision.
  5. Practise those topics and attempt another paper to check your progress.
  6. Check official notifications for current exam dates, eligibility rules and instructions.

Frequently Asked Questions about HTET

Where can I find official HTET previous year papers?

Use the year-wise BSEH links listed above. The official pages show the papers made available by the board.

Are the papers available as direct downloads on PYQ Hub?

No. These links open the official BSEH website. PYQ Hub currently directs visitors to the board's pages rather than hosting its own PDF copies.

How should I choose the correct HTET level?

Check the current BSEH eligibility requirements and the teaching level relevant to your intended post before selecting an examination level.

Why should I practise previous year papers?

They provide practice with past questions and help you identify topics that may need more revision. They do not guarantee that the same questions will appear in a future examination.

Where can I check official HTET updates?

Visit the Board of School Education Haryana website for official notices, syllabus details and examination updates.
