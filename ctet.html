How to Prepare Using Previous Year Papers

  1. Review the latest official CTET syllabus.
  2. Attempt a previous year paper under timed conditions.
  3. Check your answers and identify topics needing revision.
  4. Practise weak topics and attempt another paper.
  5. Verify exam dates and instructions on the official CTET website.
